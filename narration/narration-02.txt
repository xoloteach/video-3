Every option creates another question.

Should I choose this?

Or that?

What if there's something better?

What if I make the wrong decision?

What if I regret it?

And suddenly…

choosing becomes work.

Think about something as simple as watching a movie.

If you only have five movies…

you choose one.

But if you have five hundred…

you start searching.

You read reviews.

You watch trailers.

You compare ratings.

You look at what other people are watching.

And after twenty minutes…

you haven't watched anything.

The problem wasn't a lack of entertainment.

The problem was too much choice.

And this happens everywhere.

Especially with your life.

You don't just have one career path anymore.

You have thousands.

You can become a designer.

A programmer.

A doctor.

A lawyer.

A creator.

A freelancer.

A businessman.

A trader.

A YouTuber.

A consultant.

You can work remotely.

Move to another country.

Start a company.

Build an online business.

Learn artificial intelligence.

Learn marketing.

Learn editing.

Learn coding.

Learn sales.

And every day…

the internet reminds you that someone is doing all of these things.

Someone is making money from YouTube.

Someone is travelling the world.

Someone built a company at twenty.

Someone learned a new skill in three months.

Someone is already living the life you are still trying to figure out.

And that's where another problem begins.

FOMO.

The fear of missing out.

Because when you have unlimited options…

committing to one option means saying no to thousands of others.
