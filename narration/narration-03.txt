And your brain doesn't like that.

Imagine you decide to focus on learning one skill for the next year.

Sounds simple.

But then you see someone online talking about another skill.

It looks more profitable.

So you start thinking…

Maybe I should learn that instead.

Then you see another opportunity.

Then another.

Then another.

And suddenly…

you're not building anything.

You're constantly switching.

Not because you're incapable.

But because you're afraid that somewhere…

there is a better option.

And this is one of the biggest traps of modern life.

We think keeping every door open gives us freedom.

But sometimes…

keeping every door open prevents us from walking through any of them.

Think about your attention.

Your attention is limited.

You only have so many hours in a day.

You only have so much mental energy.

You can spend that energy thinking about hundreds of possibilities…

or you can spend it building one thing.

And every time you switch…

there is a cost.

You have to remember where you stopped.

Understand the new task.

Adjust your thinking.

Get motivated again.

Start over.

And then…

switch again.

This is why you can spend an entire day feeling busy…

without actually moving forward.

You answered messages.

Watched videos.

Researched ideas.

Saved posts.

Made plans.

Changed your goals.

Downloaded a new productivity app.

Maybe even watched a video about discipline.

And at the end of the day…

you feel exhausted.

But you can't point to anything meaningful that you actually finished.
