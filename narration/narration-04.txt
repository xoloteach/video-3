Because you were active…

but not focused.

And focus is not simply the ability to concentrate.

Focus is the ability to ignore.

To say…

not this.

Not now.

Maybe later.

I'm choosing this one thing.

That sounds easy.

But it's incredibly difficult in a world designed to give you more.

More content.

More entertainment.

More products.

More information.

More opportunities.

More opinions.

More people telling you what you should do with your life.

And the strange thing is…

we often mistake more information for more progress.

We think if we keep researching…

we will eventually find the perfect answer.

The perfect career.

The perfect business.

The perfect strategy.

The perfect routine.

The perfect opportunity.

But sometimes…

research becomes another form of procrastination.

Because as long as you're researching…

you don't have to commit.

You can keep imagining different futures.

And every future looks possible.

But none of them become real.

This is why constraints can actually be powerful.

A constraint is simply a limit.

Instead of asking…

What could I possibly do?

You ask…

What am I going to do?

Instead of ten goals…

choose two.

Instead of learning five skills…

choose one.

Instead of trying every strategy…

choose one and stay with it long enough to see what happens.

Instead of asking…

What's the best possible option?

Ask…

What's good enough for me to move forward?

Because perfection has a hidden cost.

The more you try to optimize every decision…

the more time you spend deciding.
