How can I do more?

What can I stop doing?

You cannot learn every skill.

You cannot build every business.

You cannot take every opportunity.

And that's okay.

You don't need every possible life.

You need to build one.

That means closing some doors.

Saying no to some opportunities.

Accepting that you might miss out.

Choosing something…

and giving it enough time to become meaningful.

Because sometimes…

the freedom to choose everything…

is exactly what keeps you from choosing anything.

Your focus becomes stronger when your options become smaller.

Your progress becomes faster when your direction becomes clearer.

And your life becomes calmer when you stop asking…

What else could I do?

What have I decided to do?

You don't need more options.

less choice doesn't mean less freedom.

Sometimes…

it's the thing that finally gives you freedom.
