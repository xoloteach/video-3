## Description
Too many choices can make deciding harder—especially when the options are complex, your preferences are unclear, or you feel pressure to choose perfectly. This video explores choice overload, switching costs, FoMO, and the practical power of constraints: fewer goals, clearer priorities, and decisions you make once instead of every day.

The research is nuanced: more options are not automatically worse. But when every possibility competes for your attention, keeping every door open can make it harder to walk through any of them.
